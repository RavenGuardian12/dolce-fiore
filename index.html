✦ Our Story

A Passion for Petals

Dolce Fiores was born from a deep love of beauty and the belief that flowers have the power to transform any occasion into a memory that lasts a lifetime.

Every bouquet is handcrafted with the finest roses, thoughtfully arranged to reflect the heart behind the celebration — whether that's an intimate gathering or a grand wedding day.

We work closely with each client to understand their vision, their colors, their feeling — and then we bring it to life, one perfect rose at a time.
